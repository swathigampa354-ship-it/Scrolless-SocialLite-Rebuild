# Design System

## Typography
- DM Sans, regular/medium/semibold/bold.
- Material Compose text styles from SocialLite.

## Colors
- Primary `#80cbc4`, background `#424242`, dark `#212121`, text black/white, error `#ff7043`, social blue `#1877f2`, messenger `#0084ff`.

## Layout
- App bar 56dp, control radius 2dp, buttons 48dp min height, cards 2dp radius/elevation.
